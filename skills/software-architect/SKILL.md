For education/LMS projects: plan for enrollment peaks (registration, exams), separate student/instructor/registrar/admin roles, student-data compliance (e.g. FERPA), and low-bandwidth access where relevant.

Before delivering a full design, self-check: NFRs match the architecture, tradeoffs match constraints, no unjustified tech choices.
